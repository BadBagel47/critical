Online play and what is sent

Critical ST8 can be played alone without anything leaving your browser. This page says what happens when you play online, or report a play test.

Playing online

  • Rooms run on Cloudflare, in the EU. One player's browser runs the game; the others send what they press and get the steps of the game back.
  • What is sent: the name you choose, the room's code, what you press, and the game itself when you join. Nothing else about you.
  • Cloudflare handles your IP address to carry the connection, as any website does.
  • A room keeps the names of those in it, a random key for each browser (stored only as a hash), and the latest state of the game. It is deleted 24 hours after the last player has left, or an hour after it was closed.

In your browser

  • Saved games, the sound settings and the name you chose for online play are kept in your browser's own storage, and never sent anywhere unless you play online.
  • When you play online, every player keeps a copy of the crew's saves, so that anyone can go on another day.

Play tests

  • Only in a browser that has opened a tester link from the game's maker: the answers you give, any pictures you add, and the page's context (address, device, browser, window size, errors, frames per second) are sent with the report.
  • Reports are kept for 30 days, unless the maker keeps one longer.
